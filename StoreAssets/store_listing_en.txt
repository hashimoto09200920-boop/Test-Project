【Full description】(max 4000 chars)
Draw a line. Bounce the bullets back!

NEON DANCER is a 2D action game where you draw lines with your finger to reflect enemy bullets and take down your foes.
Protect the dancer who never stops dancing on neon-lit stages, and challenge the bosses waiting at the end of each area.

■ Draw lines to attack and defend
Just trace the screen to draw a line of light.
Straight lines, zigzags, curves, circles — any shape you like. Block bullets to stay safe, or send them flying back at your enemies. It all depends on how you draw.

■ Master two kinds of lines
The "White Line" is easy to draw again and again. The "Red Line" costs more, but it is stronger and harder to break.
Choosing the right line for the moment is the key to victory.

■ Just Reflect, Circles, and Slow Motion
Draw a line right before a bullet hits for a "Just Reflect" and deal double damage. Trap enemies and bullets inside a circle for nonstop hits.
Use Slow Motion to calmly redirect stray bullets or draw the perfect circle.

■ Defeat enemies and power up your skills
Clear stages to earn skill cards.
Mix and match from 22 skills to fit your play style, and get ready for the boss battles.

■ Grow stronger with Gems
Clear stages to earn Gems with random skill effects.
Equipped Gems carry over to your next runs. The more you play, the stronger you get — a roguelite twist.

■ Change your strategy with Food
Buy food at the food stall before you head out to boost your abilities for your next run.
Plan your strategy around each area and boss.

■ 10 areas and over 40 types of enemies
More than 40 types of enemies await across 10 areas.
Bullet storms, surprise warps, bullets that pierce your lines and more — read their attacks and draw your way through.

■ A friendly tutorial for first-time players
Learn step by step as you play, from drawing lines to Just Reflect and trapping enemies in circles.

【Recommended for】
- Players who love satisfying touch-controlled action games
- Fans of bullet-dodging shooters and rhythm-style games
- Players who enjoy building characters by combining skills
